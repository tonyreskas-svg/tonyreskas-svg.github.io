Three pieces of work that show how I operate

Each one started with a planning or decision problem and ended with a measurable change in how the business runs.

An operating model built from scratch

The problem
Servicing Engineering had data in several systems but no shared way to decide where effort and investment should go.
What I did
Designed a six-pillar framework with a north star and supporting metrics per pillar, set the governance rhythm, and took performance, priorities and ROI to the SVP of Operations on a regular cadence.
Outcome
The framework is now the basis for how the function prioritises resources and investment.

Predictive failure detection

The problem
Software and hardware issues surfaced only after robots failed in the field, driving spare parts cost, taking robots out of service, and reaching product teams too late to shape the next release.
What I did
Led a tool that uses robot telemetry and historical fleet data to flag issues before they cause failures, so the team could act before robots left service.
Outcome
~20% lower spare parts cost and ~15% higher fleet utilisation, with improved reliability.

The Technical Toolbox programme

The problem
Field technicians across two regions had no consistent diagnostic platform or knowledge at the point of need.
What I did
Ran it from requirements to deployment: specification, plan, budget and API integrations, with 3 software and 3 servicing engineers over about 12 months.
Outcome
Rolled out to 100+ technicians across the US and EU.

The metrics framework I built

The operating model I built: six pillars, each with a north star and the supporting metrics that explain it. It is how priorities, capacity and investment are argued at SLT level. Highlighted metrics are the cost, capacity and planning measures.

PillarNorth starSupporting metrics
Operational excellenceRefurbishment cycle time, SBD rateRefurbishment throughput vs target, service buffer coverage, on-time deployment rate, active fleet %, labour hours per refurb
Reliability engineeringMean distance between failures, first-time fix rateMTTR and repeat failures, preventive maintenance failure rate, recurrence of known faults
Financial efficiencyCost per refurb unit, spare parts costParts cost as % of refurb cost, swap time and unit cost vs repair
Tooling and dataReporting coverage, dashboard adoptionKPI dashboards with live data feeds, field data capture rate, Jira ticket-to-resolution cycle time, report delivery vs SLA
Strategic scalingFleet uptime, scaling milestone hit rateUS servicing model defined and validated, hub-and-spoke network efficiency, EU/US capacity vs deployment demand
Cross-functional integrationFeedback loop velocity, ops alignmentJoint OKR coverage, observation-to-change time, parts availability vs demand, field insights actioned per quarter
