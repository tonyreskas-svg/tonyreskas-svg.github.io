Three projects that show how I deliver

Two software builds and the delivery rhythm behind them, each run with engineers and stakeholders across the US and EU.

The Technical Toolbox platform

The need
Field technicians across two regions had no consistent diagnostic platform or knowledge at the point of need.
What I did
Wrote the specification, set the plan, schedule and budget, and ran Scrum delivery with 3 software and 3 servicing engineers, including API integrations with existing systems.
Outcome
Delivered in ~12 months and rolled out to 100+ users across the US and EU.

Predictive failure detection

The need
Software and hardware issues surfaced only after robots failed in the field, driving spare parts cost and reaching product teams too late.
What I did
Led a tool that uses robot telemetry and historical fleet data to flag issues before they cause failures, so teams could act before robots left service.
Outcome
~20% lower spare parts cost and ~15% higher fleet utilisation.

A delivery rhythm people trust

The need
Several concurrent initiatives, data spread across systems, and no single view of progress, risk and priority.
What I did
Configured Jira around our workflows, set the review cadence with the SVP of Operations, and used AI tools to automate reporting from multiple data sources.
Outcome
One view of progress and priorities, used to decide where effort goes.

How I run a software delivery project

The practices I bring to every project, drawing on how I've delivered at Starship and Amazon.

AreaWhat I put in placeWhy it matters
Realistic plansPlans built with the people doing the work, with a stated confidence level, not just a dateTeams commit to dates they believe in
Visible progressOne Jira board and a short weekly view of progress, confidence and decisions neededStakeholders see the same picture without chasing
Risks & dependenciesA shared log with owners and dates, reviewed weekly across teams and sitesProblems surface while they are still cheap to fix
Recovery when off trackRe-plan scope and sequence with the team, agree it with stakeholders, then track the recovery plan visiblyTrust is rebuilt quickly instead of drifting
